CUEVOLUTION
About Us

Revolutionizing pool. Building the future.

Cuevolution Africa is a sports and cuesports company on a mission to popularize, professionalize, and transform the sport of pool in Kenya and across Africa. We believe pool is more than a game — it's a sport with the potential to create careers, build communities, develop young talent, generate businesses, and provide sustainable livelihoods across the continent.

What We Do

Building the ecosystem around the sport.

From quality infrastructure and equipment to player development, business opportunities, events, and industry collaboration.

Pool Tables & Equipment

A leading supplier of professional pool tables and equipment in East Africa — including United King, Joy Heyball and Rasson tables — with delivery, installation, maintenance and technical support.

Pool Venue Development

We help businesses and investors establish and improve pool venues — from table selection to layout — transforming spaces into professionally managed, sustainable hubs for the sport.

Events & Tournament Management

Planning, promotion and management of pool tournaments — registration, formats and scheduling, branding, livestreaming, sponsorship activation, and prize management.

Player Development & Talent Management

Identifying and supporting emerging players with pathways for training, mentorship, competition and exposure — from grassroots to national and international competition.

Consultancy

Practical industry insight for clubs, venues, investors, federations and organizers — from setting up a facility to developing a tournament or a pool-based business model.

Beyond The Table

Building an African pool industry.

Cuevolution Africa's ambition extends beyond selling pool tables. We want to contribute towards building a strong, organized, and professional pool industry in Africa. We believe the future of the sport requires greater collaboration between players, clubs, businesses, manufacturers, event organizers, federations, sponsors, media, and other stakeholders.

We seek to bring stakeholders together, encourage dialogue, lobby for the development of the sport, and contribute ideas through partnerships and industry led initiatives — including our long term vision of a pool industry think tank that can identify challenges, develop solutions, share knowledge, and advocate for the growth and professionalization of cuesports across the region.

The growth of pool creates opportunities far beyond the players at the table. A thriving pool ecosystem can support livelihoods for venue owners, coaches, referees, tournament organizers, technicians, table installers, cloth fitters, content creators, broadcasters, marketers, event staff, equipment suppliers, and other professionals. We want to see pool evolve from a recreational activity into a recognized sporting and commercial ecosystem capable of creating meaningful opportunities for Africans.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading force in the development, professionalization, and commercialization of pool and cuesports.

Our Mission

To grow the sport by improving infrastructure, developing talent, supporting businesses, creating world class events, connecting stakeholders, and opening new opportunities throughout the African pool ecosystem.
