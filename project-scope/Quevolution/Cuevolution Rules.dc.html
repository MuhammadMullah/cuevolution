How it works

Tournament rules

The current format and stage structure Cuevolution runs on. This page reflects our best format ahead of final confirmation from tournament directors — check back for updates.

Format & categories

Cuevolution runs standard 8-ball pool. Players register into one of three categories: Individual Male, Individual Female, or Teams. Matches are best-of-a-set race format, with racking and break order alternating between racks unless a venue's house rules state otherwise.

The four stages

Grassroots

Open entry, grouped by venue. Each group plays a round robin — no knockouts at this stage. The top finishers from each group (an admin-configurable number, typically the top 2) advance to Regional.

Regional

Grassroots qualifiers are grouped by region and again play a round robin — still no knockouts. Top finishers advance to the Circuit.

Circuit

The first knockout stage — a single bracket per category, open to qualifiers from any region. Capacity: 128 Individual Male · 64 Individual Female · 20 Teams. Cuevo Points begin accruing here.

Finals

The national knockout stage. Capacity: 64 Individual Male · 32 Individual Female · 8 Teams. Cuevo Points here decide the season's champions.

Scoring & Cuevo Points

Standings track matches played, win–loss record and — from the Circuit stage onward — Cuevo Points, which determine seeding and final rankings. Grassroots and Regional results decide advancement only; Cuevo Points start at zero for every entrant until Circuit.

Conduct

Cuevolution is built on fair play and good sportsmanship. Players are expected to respect opponents, venues and officials at every stage. Disputes over a recorded result should be raised with your venue organiser as soon as possible.
